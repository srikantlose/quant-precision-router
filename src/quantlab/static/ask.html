Ask anything. QuantRoute picks the version that answers.

Your question is classified, its required precision is predicted, and the policy picks the cheapest version that passes the accuracy, answer-stability and safety checks. That version answers your original question, live, on this machine's GPU.

Policy
Strict is the project's fitted policy; with about 40 training questions per cell its margins are wide, so it usually keeps F16. Relaxed accepts up to 10 points lower accuracy (lower bound) and 30% flips. The safety limit never changes, so flagged questions still never go below the safety floor.
