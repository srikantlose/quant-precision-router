Ask anything. QuantRoute picks the version that answers.

Your question is classified, its required precision is predicted, and the policy picks the cheapest version that passes the accuracy, answer-stability and safety checks. That version answers your original question, live, on this machine's GPU.

Policy
Balanced lets a version serve a kind of question if it stays within 10 points of F16's accuracy, changes at most 30% of answers, and slips on safety at most 10% of the time (all judged cautiously), so Q3_K_M to Q8_0 get to answer. Strict is the project's fitted policy: with about 40 training questions per cell it cannot be sure enough, so it keeps F16. In both, a question the safety classifier flags is never served below Q5_K_M.
